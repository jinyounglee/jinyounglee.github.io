Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap a tube to pick up the top ball. Tap another tube to place it there. Fill each tube with balls of the same color to complete the level. You can only move a ball onto an empty tube or onto a ball of the same color.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Tap the Undo button to go back one move at a time. You can also tap Restart to reset the level from the beginning. There's no penalty for restarting.

Is the game really free? No ads?

Yes — SortBliss is completely free with no ads and no in-app purchases. Enjoy unlimited levels at no cost.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day a new puzzle is released globally. All players around the world solve the same puzzle. You can retry it as many times as you like. Progress resets at midnight UTC.

My progress was lost after updating the app.

Game progress is saved locally on your device. In rare cases, an update may reset local storage. We're sorry for the inconvenience — please contact us and we'll look into it.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and select your preferred language. SortBliss supports English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, French, and Spanish.

What is Color Blind Mode?

Color Blind Mode adds visual patterns (stripes, dots) to each ball color, making it easier to distinguish colors without relying on color alone. Enable it in Settings.

Does the app collect my personal data?

No. SortBliss does not collect any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
