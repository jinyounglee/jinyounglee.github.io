Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does BlockPop require an internet connection?

No! BlockPop works completely offline. All your progress and scores are saved locally on your device.

Q: How do I earn coins?

You earn coins by clearing lines during gameplay, completing daily challenges, and watching rewarded ads. Coins can be used to purchase power-ups and unlock themes in the Shop.

Q: What are the power-ups?

BlockPop offers three power-ups: Bomb (clears a 3×3 area), Undo (reverts your last move), and Shuffle (rearranges your current pieces). You can buy them with coins in the Shop.

Q: How does the Daily Challenge work?

A new puzzle appears every day at midnight (UTC). Complete challenges to earn bonus coins and build a streak. The longer your streak, the greater the rewards!

Q: How do I change the theme?

Go to the Shop tab and browse the Themes section. You can unlock and apply 5 different themes: Classic, Cool Dark, Forest, Ocean, and Candy.

Q: Can I remove ads?

Ad-free mode is not available in the current version. We're working on adding this option in a future update. Ads never interrupt you during active gameplay.

Q: My progress was lost. Can I recover it?

Game data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, your progress may be lost. We recommend keeping the app installed to preserve your data. Cloud sync is planned for a future update.

Q: Is BlockPop available on Android?

BlockPop is currently available on iOS. Android support is coming soon!

This privacy policy describes how BlockPop ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.

  • Advertising Data: Advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) for serving personalized ads, subject to your consent.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.

All game data (scores, settings, progress) is stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not transmit or store your game data on external servers. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be deleted.

The App displays ads through Google AdMob, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads. You may opt out of personalized advertising:

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
